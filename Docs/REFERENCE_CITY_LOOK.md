# City reference direction — 8 October 2026

The seven user-provided images guide environment presentation: layered masonry buildings, readable floor divisions, detailed roofs, warm directional light and cool shadows, with blue night atmosphere and warm window lights. Landscape orientation is preserved.

This pass adds projecting architectural courses, facade columns aligned with window bay boundaries, masonry rooftop parapets and compact rooftop HVAC equipment with grille detail, away from stair circulation. The enclosure and parapets provide physical cover; cosmetic grilles are batched with the building. Upper shells now use window facades with UV repetition based on physical metres and mapped storey heights, rather than blank or stretched upper walls. Actual entry and firing openings remain clear.

Clear-day missions use lower, warmer sunlight, cooler ambient fill, subdued saturation and modest contrast. Day sky colours support the warmer horizon. Existing night window/street lighting, rain and environment selection remain active. Captured day/night views use the runtime environment settings and a cached real-map sector.

Weapon handling also now has critically damped recoil, smoother movement and aiming transitions, staged reload poses, support-hand reach/withdraw/reseat movement and bolt-action hand cycling. Weapon switches reset motion state. These are procedural handling poses using the existing arms; this pass does not add authored magazine or moving bolt meshes.

Validation covers recoil recovery at 30/60/120fps, hand poses on all eight weapons, source compilation, real-sector rendering and campaign setup/indoor interaction checks. Reports/previews: `output/reference-city/` and `output/weapon-handling/`.
