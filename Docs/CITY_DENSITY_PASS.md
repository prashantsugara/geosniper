# City density pass

Fictional background architecture fills the horizon beyond the playable sector: up to 72 towers in four batched district meshes, varied height/width and some setback crowns. Buildings have no collision or shadows and do not enter mapped features, navigation, objectives or building pins. The first loaded sector creates the backdrop; streamed sectors do not duplicate it. Water rejection uses the currently known water data; this is not guaranteed geographic accuracy beyond downloaded coverage.

Parks have increased tree sampling probability (.09 to .16), within existing total tree budgets and placement checks. This does not replace park land with buildings or increase the global vegetation budget.

Compilation passed. Device visuals and performance have not been measured. This is procedural scenery, not parity with the advert's fully detailed skyline or proof of AAA quality.
