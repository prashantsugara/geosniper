Its existing 8–24 metre heights are retained, and deterministic footprint proportions stay within the earlier .8 scale limit for playable interiors. Fictional distant scenery now has varied 18–174 metre silhouettes and physically scaled, illuminated windows instead of plain unlit boxes.
