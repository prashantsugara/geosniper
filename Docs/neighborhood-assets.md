# Neighborhood scenery and vehicle pass

Roadside scenery now includes six scenes: paired vehicle parking, market stalls with produce and supply crates, bus shelters with benches, pallet and cargo stacks, utility work zones, and planter seating. These supplement the existing cars, military props, trees, streetlights and building details.

The generator prioritizes roads within 220 metres of the entry focus. Desktop allows up to 42 accepted scenes and 14 extra vehicles; mobile allows up to 18 scenes and six vehicles. Dense locations can accept fewer scenes. Existing validated Audi, Ford, BMW and police vehicle models are reused.

The 6 October pass adds downloaded Street Props by syedraza from Sketchfab through Blender MCP on port 9876. Five FBX models (bench, bin, crate, cone and drum) use three shared 1024px atlases, with grounded meshes and uniform game-scale sizing. Bins accompany accepted sites; crates appear near markets and deliveries; cones and drums mark utility work; benches sit by garden seating. These decorative props retain their imported UVs and material slots, and are excluded from primitive batching. Existing bounded site cover supplies collisions. Attribution is in game Asset Credits and Store/SketchfabNeighborhoodLicense.json. CGTrader's Free Street Props listing was checked, but downloading remained blocked by the signed-out browser.

Placement avoids mapped buildings, water, intersecting roads, steep ground, occupied collider footprints and the immediate entry area. Accepted sites have at least 14 metres between their centers. Street furniture is combined by material into meshes, and generation yields between sites. Cars, counters, cargo stacks, planters and work barriers use bounded physical colliders for combat cover. The navigation mesh is built after placement.

Validation: runtime and editor compilation through `Tools/check_code.ps1`; isolated Unity `NeighborhoodDressingChecks.Run`. The synthetic three-road sector produced 42 scenes, 14 vehicles and all six scene types. Checks covered travel-lane clearance, collider dimensions, active batched renderers and a usable player entry spawn. Preview: `output/neighborhood/preview.png`. Android frame time and real-location density require device playtesting.

Downloaded prop validation passed in isolated Unity: all five prop types instantiated, atlas textures present, imported mesh bounds under five metres, lane clearance and entry spawn retained.
