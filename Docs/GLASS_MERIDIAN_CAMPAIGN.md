# Operation Glass Meridian

A 50-mission campaign in five chapters. Rook, handler Mara, cartographer Mina and defecting marksman Ghost uncover a fabricated evacuation crisis orchestrated by Director Vale. Rook also searches for his missing brother Eli. The map location is chosen by the player; the characters, organizations and events are fictional.

Each mission has an objective, story briefing and aftermath. Activities include marked-target shooting, VIP overwatch, courier interception, stealth, three-wave extraction defense, evidence recovery with extraction, relay sabotage with converging patrols, witness rescue followed by escort, and five rival marksman duels. Optional precision, stealth, speed or survival challenges award 10% extra credits and XP. Campaign IDs and existing completion saves remain intact; new unlocks follow the story in order.

For field actions, approach the marker, look toward it, tap START or hold E, and stay within reach until the action completes. Evidence takes 1.5 seconds; relays and witness releases take 3 seconds. Finishing the interaction chain does not bypass extraction. Witnesses stay in place until released, then follow their protected escape route.

## Chapter 1: The Wrong Address

### 1. THE WRONG ADDRESS

**Activity:** TargetIdentification

Mara sends you, callsign Rook, to stop a weapons scout. His orders name a building that does not exist. Someone is rewriting the city's map. Take the shot, then leave the sector.

**Objective:** Identify and eliminate the marked weapons scout.

**Aftermath:** The scout carried a forged evacuation route. Cartographer Mina says the error was deliberate.

### 2. THE CARTOGRAPHER

**Activity:** Overwatch

Mina can prove the evacuation map is false. A private militia called Meridian is hunting her. Cover her escape and keep her alive; she knows where the original survey is hidden.

**Objective:** Protect Mina until she reaches the extraction vehicle.

**Aftermath:** Mina escapes. The forged routes all pass Meridian checkpoints.

### 3. RED ENVELOPE

**Activity:** TimedInterception

A Meridian courier is carrying the checkpoint schedule out of the district. Mara needs that schedule before the militia changes it. Watch the bends in his route and intercept him before he leaves.

**Objective:** Intercept the marked courier before he reaches the exit.

**Aftermath:** The schedule points to an abandoned survey office. Every page bears the same symbol: a broken compass.

### 4. QUIET SURVEY

**Activity:** Stealth

The survey office is watched by Meridian sentries. Clear its security ring without triggering the alarm. Mina needs time to compare the original street records with the forged routes.

**Objective:** Eliminate the sentries before the alarm reaches its limit.

**Aftermath:** Mina finds that the map was altered using a government access key.

### 5. CARBON COPIES

**Activity:** EvidenceRecovery

The office records are being scattered across nearby caches. Recover both copies and leave with the proof. Meridian guards are expendable to their employer; the evidence is what matters.

**Objective:** Recover two marked evidence caches, then reach the exit.

**Aftermath:** The copies name a signal contractor and a payment account registered to Director Vale.

### 6. DEAD AIR

**Activity:** Sabotage

Meridian's relay lets its spotters share your position. Disable both local terminals. The first outage will alert their patrols, so choose cover before you start the second shutdown.

**Objective:** Disable both relay terminals, then withdraw to the exit.

**Aftermath:** A stranded operator sends one last message: 'Ghost was ordered to erase the witnesses.'

### 7. BROKEN COMPASS

**Activity:** TargetIdentification

The checkpoint commander is selling civilians a false safe route. His rooftop lookout protects the operation. Remove the commander and any counter-sniper so Mina can warn the district.

**Objective:** Eliminate the marked commander and his counter-sniper.

**Aftermath:** The commander kept photographs of missing survey workers, including your brother Eli.

### 8. A WITNESS ALIVE

**Activity:** Rescue

Jo, the relay technician, survived the shutdown. Meridian has cornered him before he can testify. Reach him, hold your position to release him, then cover his run to extraction.

**Objective:** Reach and release Jo, then protect his escape.

**Aftermath:** Jo says Ghost refused an order to kill Eli. The marksman may be an enemy you can turn.

### 9. HOLD THE LINE

**Activity:** Escape

Meridian found Jo's evacuation channel. Hold against the assault teams while Mara brings in a helicopter. Reload between waves and move to extraction only when the landing zone opens.

**Objective:** Survive three waves, then board the extraction helicopter.

**Aftermath:** Jo gets out, but Ghost challenges you on an open frequency. He will speak only after you beat him.

### 10. GHOST'S TERMS

**Activity:** RivalDuel

Ghost believes you are Vale's executioner. Break his firing locks and defeat him in a sniper duel. You need his testimony more than another name on the casualty list.

**Objective:** Defeat Ghost in the rival sniper duel.

**Aftermath:** Ghost yields and sends a shipping manifest. Eli is alive, held by Meridian's logistics division.


## Chapter 2: Paper Trail

### 11. THE MANIFEST

**Activity:** EvidenceRecovery

Ghost's manifest is encrypted in two separate records. Reach the marked caches and extract both. Mina can trace the prisoner transfer only if the pages stay together.

**Objective:** Recover both manifest records and reach the exit.

**Aftermath:** The manifest hides prisoner transfers inside medical freight payments.

### 12. MEDICAL COVER

**Activity:** Overwatch

Dr. Imani treated the transferred prisoners. Meridian intends to silence her before she reaches Mara's vehicle. Give her a clear route and prioritize the attackers closing on her.

**Objective:** Keep Dr. Imani alive until extraction.

**Aftermath:** Imani confirms Eli was moved after refusing to design false evacuation routes.

### 13. THE PAYMASTER

**Activity:** TargetIdentification

Meridian's paymaster is meeting the transport guards. End his operation and remove his lookout. His encrypted receipts will expose who funds the false checkpoints.

**Objective:** Eliminate the paymaster and any counter-sniper.

**Aftermath:** The receipts connect Vale's office to a private emergency-services contract.

### 14. NO SIGNAL HOME

**Activity:** Sabotage

The paymaster's guards are forwarding the receipts to Vale. Shut down three relay terminals before that network recovers. Expect the surviving guards to rush your position after each shutdown.

**Objective:** Disable three relay terminals and reach the exit.

**Aftermath:** Mina intercepts a backup address before the relays fall silent.

### 15. THE SECOND COURIER

**Activity:** TimedInterception

A courier has the backup key. Ghost will watch the rooftops while you track the runner through the street corridor. Stop him before the key reaches the transport division.

**Objective:** Intercept the courier before his exit or the timer expires.

**Aftermath:** The key opens a transfer record signed by Warden, Meridian's prison marksman.

### 16. EMPTY BEDS

**Activity:** Stealth

The transfer station is empty, but its guards are still sweeping for witnesses. Clear the sentries quietly. A loud alarm will give Warden enough warning to move the remaining prisoners.

**Objective:** Clear the sentries without triggering lockdown.

**Aftermath:** The prisoners left minutes ago. One guard's radio is still connected to Warden.

### 17. SOMEONE LEFT BEHIND

**Activity:** Rescue

A driver named Sam was left behind after the transfer. Reach him before the guards finish their search, release him and cover his escape. He remembers which vehicle carried Eli.

**Objective:** Release Sam and protect him to extraction.

**Aftermath:** Sam saw Eli enter a vehicle marked 'Civic Survey'. Mina recognizes the company.

### 18. CIVIC SURVEY

**Activity:** EvidenceRecovery

Civic Survey stores its contracts in three field caches. Collect them and leave with the files. The company is a shell; finding the person behind it matters more than clearing every patrol.

**Objective:** Collect three contract files and reach the exit.

**Aftermath:** The contracts describe Glass Meridian: a manufactured crisis followed by a paid 'rescue'.

### 19. BURN NOTICE

**Activity:** Escape

Vale has ordered the evidence destroyed and your team erased. Defend the evacuation zone through successive assaults. Mina needs every second of the hold to copy the contracts off the device.

**Objective:** Survive the assault waves and board the helicopter.

**Aftermath:** The contracts survive. Warden blocks the next transfer route and demands a duel.

### 20. WARDEN'S WATCH

**Activity:** RivalDuel

Warden controls the prisoner route from high ground. Ghost has given you his firing habits: a fast lock, then a pause to reposition. Use cover, break the lock and win the duel.

**Objective:** Defeat Warden and reopen the prisoner route.

**Aftermath:** Warden's radio reveals a disturbing detail: Vale has been reading Mara's dispatches.


## Chapter 3: The Leak

### 21. THE LEAK

**Activity:** EvidenceRecovery

Mara fears someone inside her team is feeding Vale. Recover both dispatch logs and get out. Mina will compare the timestamps instead of trusting another accusation.

**Objective:** Recover two dispatch logs and reach the exit.

**Aftermath:** The leak is upstream of Mara. Vale's office has a copy of every mission order.

### 22. SWITCHBOARD

**Activity:** Sabotage

Three access terminals are forwarding Mara's orders. Disconnect them while the team changes its communications keys. Once the first terminal falls, the patrols will know where to look.

**Objective:** Disable all three forwarding terminals and withdraw.

**Aftermath:** Mara takes the team off the official channel. For the first time, Vale cannot see your next move.

### 23. OFF THE RECORD

**Activity:** Overwatch

An auditor named Nia has traced Vale's access key. Her escort was compromised, so you must cover her on foot. Keep her alive until she reaches Mara's unmarked extraction vehicle.

**Objective:** Protect Nia through the street route to extraction.

**Aftermath:** Nia brings proof that Vale created the access key himself.

### 24. THE FALSE HUNTER

**Activity:** TargetIdentification

Vale has sent a hunter wearing your team's insignia. His orders are to kill witnesses and blame Rook. Identify the marked commander and remove his rooftop support before he reaches another checkpoint.

**Objective:** Eliminate the impersonator and his counter-sniper.

**Aftermath:** The false insignia is part of Vale's plan to make the real rescue team look responsible.

### 25. THE LAST RECEIPT

**Activity:** TimedInterception

Nia found one missing receipt. Its carrier is leaving with the only record linking Vale to Meridian's assault units. Stop the courier before the document disappears into the next district.

**Objective:** Intercept the receipt courier before he escapes.

**Aftermath:** The receipt names Viper, the sniper hired to make the forged crisis look real.

### 26. LISTENING POSTS

**Activity:** Stealth

Viper's spotters are tracking Mina's new channel. Clear their sentries before the alarm peaks. Ghost warns that their overlapping patrols will punish a careless second shot.

**Objective:** Clear the listening-post sentries without lockdown.

**Aftermath:** Mina finds an old recording of Eli refusing Vale's order. She wants to publish it.

### 27. THE RECORDING

**Activity:** EvidenceRecovery

Eli's recording has two damaged fragments. Recover both field backups and reach the exit. Mina can restore the audio, but only if you bring back the original data.

**Objective:** Collect both recording backups and extract.

**Aftermath:** Eli's voice confirms the plan: close real routes, advertise false ones, then sell the rescue.

### 28. DON'T TRUST THE BADGE

**Activity:** Rescue

Nia's cover has been blown. Meridian's 'rescue' team is holding her for a staged transfer. Reach her yourself, release her and protect her until Mara's vehicle arrives.

**Objective:** Release Nia and escort her to the real extraction.

**Aftermath:** Nia identifies the first emergency district Vale plans to seal.

### 29. CUT THE FEED

**Activity:** Escape

Publishing Eli's recording exposes your sector. Hold against the incoming waves while Mina finishes the upload. The helicopter will not enter until the approach is clear.

**Objective:** Hold through three waves, then board extraction.

**Aftermath:** The recording reaches the public. Viper starts hunting the team personally.

### 30. VIPER'S CROSSHAIR

**Activity:** RivalDuel

Viper intends to end the story with your death. His lock is shorter than Warden's; a long peek is a losing trade. Defeat him so Mina can move the evidence into the threatened district.

**Objective:** Defeat Viper in the rival sniper duel.

**Aftermath:** Viper's orders reveal that Vale has accelerated Glass Meridian. The crisis begins tonight.


## Chapter 4: City Under Siege

### 31. CITY UNDER SIEGE

**Activity:** Overwatch

A dispatcher named Ada is carrying the real evacuation routes. Keep her alive through the contested corridor. Each attacker you stop buys the district another safe way out.

**Objective:** Protect Ada until she reaches extraction.

**Aftermath:** Ada restores the first evacuation route. Vale sends assault units to close it again.

### 32. LOCKOUT

**Activity:** Sabotage

Meridian is using three local terminals to issue false closure notices. Shut them down and leave before the patrols regroup. Mina will replace the notices with Ada's verified routes.

**Objective:** Disable three closure terminals and withdraw.

**Aftermath:** The notices disappear. Vale switches to a courier network to keep the false orders moving.

### 33. ORDERS ON PAPER

**Activity:** TimedInterception

A courier is carrying the next closure order to the militia. Intercept him while the streets are still open. His escorts are distractions; the marked runner is your objective.

**Objective:** Stop the closure courier before he reaches the exit.

**Aftermath:** The order identifies the commander coordinating the district siege.

### 34. THE SIEGE COMMANDER

**Activity:** TargetIdentification

The commander controls the assault teams closing Ada's corridor. Remove him and any rooftop marksman covering the position. Mara needs a clear approach for a witness extraction.

**Objective:** Eliminate the siege commander and his counter-sniper.

**Aftermath:** The commander falls. A survey crew emerges from hiding and asks for help.

### 35. SURVEY CREW

**Activity:** Rescue

Crew leader Len knows where Eli was moved after the first transfer. Reach him, release him from cover and protect his route out. His crew cannot survive another false rescue.

**Objective:** Release Len and protect his escape.

**Aftermath:** Len says Eli is being forced to build Vale's final route map.

### 36. FINAL ROUTES

**Activity:** EvidenceRecovery

Len hid three survey records before his capture. Recover them and extract. Mina can identify the final containment zone by comparing Eli's work with the forged evacuation plans.

**Objective:** Recover all three survey records and reach the exit.

**Aftermath:** The containment zone is the district where Vale intends to announce his paid rescue.

### 37. BEFORE THE ANNOUNCEMENT

**Activity:** Stealth

Titan's sentries protect the containment operation. Remove them quietly before Vale announces that the district is sealed. Ghost will watch for the rival sniper, but he cannot stop the patrols for you.

**Objective:** Clear the security ring without triggering lockdown.

**Aftermath:** The team reaches the containment perimeter before Vale's announcement.

### 38. A WAY THROUGH

**Activity:** Sabotage

Two control terminals are coordinating the perimeter patrols. Disable both and withdraw to the exit. The second terminal sits in the same network; expect the first outage to draw a response.

**Objective:** Disable both perimeter terminals and reach the exit.

**Aftermath:** Mina opens a verified corridor through the containment zone.

### 39. THE OPEN CORRIDOR

**Activity:** Escape

Meridian is trying to close Mina's corridor by force. Hold the extraction sector while the team gets the witnesses out. Clear each wave before the next approach overwhelms you.

**Objective:** Survive three assault waves and board the helicopter.

**Aftermath:** The witnesses escape through a real route. Titan challenges the sniper who ruined his perimeter.

### 40. TITAN FALLS

**Activity:** RivalDuel

Titan controls the last firing lane into Eli's holding sector. His heavy shots punish exposure. Make him commit to a lock, break it with cover and defeat him before moving the rescue team.

**Objective:** Defeat Titan and open the final approach.

**Aftermath:** Titan's terminal reveals Eli's holding channel. Mara finally hears your brother answer.


## Chapter 5: Last Light

### 41. ELI'S CHANNEL

**Activity:** EvidenceRecovery

Eli cannot transmit his location openly. He has hidden two signed messages in the field caches. Recover both and extract; Mina can verify that Vale has not replaced his voice with a decoy.

**Objective:** Recover both signed messages and reach the exit.

**Aftermath:** The signatures match. Eli is alive and has a copy of the original Glass Meridian contract.

### 42. NO MORE FALSE ROUTES

**Activity:** Sabotage

Vale is preparing a final wave of forged evacuation notices. Disconnect three distribution terminals. Surviving guards will converge after each outage, so do not remain exposed at the controls.

**Objective:** Disable three distribution terminals and withdraw.

**Aftermath:** The false notices stop spreading. Ada begins broadcasting verified routes instead.

### 43. THE ORIGINAL CONTRACT

**Activity:** TimedInterception

Vale is moving the original contract out with a courier. Eli's copy needs that signature record to survive scrutiny. Stop the runner before Vale can claim the evidence was fabricated.

**Objective:** Intercept the contract courier before escape.

**Aftermath:** The signature is Vale's. Mina now has enough proof to expose the entire operation.

### 44. THE FINAL GUARD

**Activity:** TargetIdentification

Vale's last field commander is covering Eli's transfer. Remove him and his counter-sniper. Mara will move only when both primary threats are down.

**Objective:** Eliminate the transfer commander and his counter-sniper.

**Aftermath:** The transfer is stopped. Eli remains in the sector, waiting for a real rescue.

### 45. BRING HIM HOME

**Activity:** Rescue

You have finally reached Eli. Move to him, hold your position to release him and cover his run to Mara's vehicle. After all the false routes, this one has to lead home.

**Objective:** Release Eli and keep him alive until extraction.

**Aftermath:** Eli reaches Mara. He gives Mina the master record of Glass Meridian.

### 46. MASTER RECORD

**Activity:** EvidenceRecovery

The master record references three independent backups. Recover each one and leave with the archive. Vale cannot bury the evidence if the team holds every version.

**Objective:** Recover three archive backups and reach the exit.

**Aftermath:** The archive is complete. Vale orders his last marksman, Eclipse, to stop the broadcast.

### 47. ONE QUIET APPROACH

**Activity:** Stealth

Eclipse's spotters are searching for Mina's transmission point. Clear them without lockdown. A quiet approach gives her time to establish the public evidence feed.

**Objective:** Clear the spotters without triggering the alarm.

**Aftermath:** Mina establishes the feed. Vale must now silence the witnesses in public.

### 48. PUBLIC WITNESS

**Activity:** Overwatch

Mina is moving the archive to Mara's extraction vehicle while the feed remains live. Protect her through the route. Losing her now would let Vale tell the ending himself.

**Objective:** Protect Mina until she reaches the extraction vehicle.

**Aftermath:** Mina gets out with the archive. The signed documents are now beyond Vale's control.

### 49. LAST LIGHT

**Activity:** Escape

Vale's remaining assault units close on your extraction sector. Hold through the waves and board the helicopter. Ghost stays on the radio; Eclipse is still watching the final route.

**Objective:** Survive the final assault waves and board extraction.

**Aftermath:** The rescue team escapes. You stay for one last confrontation so Eclipse cannot hunt them again.

### 50. GLASS MERIDIAN

**Activity:** RivalDuel

Eclipse is Vale's last weapon against the witnesses. Defeat the marksman using everything you learned: short peeks, broken locks and patient shots. The evidence is already safe. Now make the route safe too.

**Objective:** Defeat Eclipse in the final sniper duel.

**Aftermath:** Eclipse falls. Vale's signed orders reach the public and his contract is exposed. Mina restores the routes, Mara brings the witnesses home, and Eli returns to the family. Rook's final report has only three words: 'The way is clear.'


## Building access

Usable mapped buildings have marked open ground-floor doors and real firing windows. Nearby selected buildings also have multiple floors and stairs to the roof. Stair placement reserves doorway clearance; ground-floor furnishings do not obstruct entrances. Foundations extend below sampled terrain and entrance steps bridge the floor height. Small unusable footprints and raised building parts are excluded. Use FIND OPEN BUILDING to mark the nearest entrance. Reload the sector after updating the game.
