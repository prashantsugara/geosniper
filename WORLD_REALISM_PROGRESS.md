# World realism progress

Active objective: make roads, buildings, water, planting and vehicle driving as realistic as practical for the Android game. This objective is not yet complete.

## Implemented and checked
- Material tags from OSM now reach building facade, roof and road surface rendering. Shared brick, stone, timber, metal and glass textures supplement detailed facade geometry. Roof textures distinguish tile, slate, metal and tar.
- Dirt and gravel routes receive earth shoulders and omit paved lane markings/curbs.
- Water has less repetitive multi-scale ripples and a narrow animated shore foam mesh, with clipped sector boundaries and adjoining-water edges excluded.
- Dry mapped banks receive budgeted reed clusters with existing distance culling. Road and water planting exclusions remain in place.
- Vehicles use world-space road coordinates in streamed sectors, forward steering, corner speed reduction, speed-dependent braking, terrain alignment, and obstacle avoidance even during gunfire flight. Unsupported ground/water blocks movement. Cars stop at route ends rather than reversing instantly.
- Tools/check_code.ps1 checks runtime and editor compilation. The isolated Unity water-review fixture verifies geometry, shaders, bank/planting rules and actual obstacle approach/braking in normal and fleeing modes. It renders shoreline-surface.png and building-materials.png.

- Road path metadata now preserves OSM one-way/reverse and roundabout direction, tagged driving side, width-based lane placement, and exclusion of very narrow routes. Cars start from rest and reject nearby occupied spawn positions. Unknown driving side still defaults to right; regional rules and Overture direction restrictions need further work.

- Traffic routes now continue across matching mapped road nodes, obey source direction, reject height-separated connections and immediate U-turns, and prefer straight continuation. Routes are bounded to 24 road pieces / 1.5 km / 512 points for mobile. Unmapped crossings are not invented.

- Cars predict crossing conflicts from nearby headings and arrival times, yield using stable tie priority, and release after the other car clears. Height-separated roads are excluded. Unity checks cover priority asymmetry, release and height separation; dense live junctions still require validation.

- Tree crowns now have position-seeded broad/narrow proportions, asymmetric cluster positions, branch orientation and trunk lean without increasing vertex/material counts. Crown seams close consistently and ellipsoid normals account for shape. The isolated tree-silhouettes.png render confirms variation; foliage still reads as solid clusters and needs better leaf-edge detail.

- Crown meshes now add six pointed leaf blades per cluster using the existing opaque foliage material (120 extra vertices and 120 double-sided triangles per tree). Preview gloss now matches matte production foliage. Render confirms this is subtle at medium distance; a fuller close-range leaf treatment remains needed.

- Water now uses a per-body 64×64 shore-distance texture for a continuous light-bank/dark-offshore color transition. This is an estimated visual depth cue, not bathymetry. Textures are released with the sector. The rendered lake was checked after removing visible vertex-interpolation triangle artifacts.

- Water receives main-light scene shadows, including reduced sunlight highlights in shade. The water-shadows.png fixture visibly confirms an occluder shadow on the water; compilation and existing Unity checks passed. Shadow availability still follows device quality settings.

- Vehicle steering now follows a speed-dependent look-ahead point along the lane with bounded steering angle and a 2.6 m wheelbase model; body yaw depends on forward motion. A 20-second Unity simulation verifies completion of a right-angle bend within a 3.5 m centreline corridor, alongside the existing obstacle checks. Dense traffic and varied real-road geometry still need validation.

- Non-tank traffic gets paired rear brake lamps using one shared mesh/material and per-vehicle property blocks. Lamps brighten during deceleration/stopping and stop emitting after detonation. Position derives from aligned vehicle bounds; model-specific fit still needs visual review. Sampled FBX assets did not expose named wheel parts, so wheel animation remains pending.

- Actual Audi, numbered Audi variant, Ford and police assets now have front/rear fixture renders. This exposed and fixed a reversed police-car orientation and missing base calibration inheritance for numbered variants. Brake lamps were reduced and repositioned; Audi and police rear close-ups were inspected after the correction. Other model fits remain to review.

- Fixed road shoulders sitting above asphalt. Curbs and lane markings now omit portions occupied by crossing roads using a spatial index; straight continuations and tagged grade-separated roads retain their edges. OSM layer tags are preserved. Cached details now retain one-way, junction and driving-side metadata. Junction helper and cache regressions pass; live intersection meshes still need a visual review.

- Building wall UVs now follow the actual wall tangent, preventing diagonal compression. The two-row facade texture repeats once per two storeys rather than twice per storey, with roughly four-metre window bays. Detailed facades share the same storey-height calculation, OSM building levels are preserved, and close-up plain materials use metre-based tiling. UV regression checks cover diagonal walls and mapped floors; live LOD transitions remain to review.

- Added RealSectorPreview and run_real_sector_preview.ps1 to render cached real geometry through the complete production SectorWorld generator in an isolated project. A 1,600-feature sector (1,208 buildings, 298 roads) rendered successfully with 6,998 meshes / 1,652,144 triangles. This exposed asphalt with repetitive raised grain; corrected world-space two-metre texture scale, irregular grain, lower normal strength and missing normal-map tangents. The same street was rerendered and visually checked. The preview uses flat elevation and desktop settings; it does not verify Android performance or water for this dry sector.

- Road surfaces and curbs now batch by material and 64 m spatial cell, with capped vertex counts and generation yields. Original collision strips and names remain for movement/traffic. The same production sector dropped from 6,998 to 5,804 rendered meshes (1,194 fewer, 17.1%) with the exact same 1,652,144 triangles. Street render was visually compared; Android FPS/thermal impact is still unmeasured. A renderer-count breakdown is saved by RealSectorPreview.

- Added oriented footprint clearance for roadside cars/furniture against buildings, water boundaries and crossing roads. Parked cars move outside the driving surface instead of overlapping its edge. Geometry checks pass and the same real street was rerendered: the previous row of cars obstructing the bend is gone. Full pedestrian-width/parking-bay semantics and large military-prop footprints remain to handle.

- Corrected day/night window material behavior: dark reflective glazing with zero emission by day, warm occupied windows at night. Removed emissive road paint and applied footprint clearance to streetlight positions. Full production real-sector day/night renders were inspected, with explicit emission transition checks. Interior depth/curtain detail and dusk transitions remain opportunities.

- Occupied window panes now carry UVs for a shared 256×128 four-pattern curtain/blind atlas, including edge shading and illumination variation. No extra room geometry or lights were added; sector triangles remain unchanged. The real night render was inspected and an initially over-dark edge fade corrected. This remains a surface illusion, not full parallax interiors.

- Environment changes now fade window/fixture emission, glazing tint and streetlamp intensity over four seconds, retaining the existing nearest-light budget. Interrupted transitions reverse from current brightness. Production fixture verifies intermediate and reversed values. This smooths artificial lighting only; it is not yet a continuous solar/daylight simulation.

- Cached building heights now survive decoding up to 220 m (previously 60 m), and mapped floor counts support up to 80 storeys. The production preview checks a 150 m tower, the 220 m cap, and 50-floor storey spacing. Source data still determines whether a given real building has a measured height.

- Rooftop ladder rungs now share one mesh per ladder instead of one renderer per rung, retaining the rails, roof landings and climbing links. In the same 1,600-feature production sector, rung renderers fell from 2,400 to 80 and total meshes from 5,847 to 3,531 (39.6% fewer) with unchanged 1,646,428 triangles. The street image was visually checked; this is a renderer-count result, not Android FPS proof.

- Rendered a cached Naini Lake OSM sector (7 mapped features) with the full production generator. Its lake, bank and parallel shore roads are visible in the overview; this fixture still uses flat elevation, so it does not establish live hillside terrain or water-level accuracy. The street render revealed an X of white edge paint where a shallow branch joins a mapped road. Road marking strips now check both ends for junction overlap, and the junction mask clears near-parallel branch paint when segments share a mapped node. The X disappeared in the rerender; the isolated map-detail regression check passed.

- Older cached OSM water features can lack `water_id`, which previously let adjacent clipped sectors estimate different heights for one lake. Named water now reuses a level only when its world-space bounds touch, and each matched sector carries that level onward along long lakes. New location loads reset the shared-level cache. Isolated Unity checks cover three connected sectors and a distant same-name lake; real hillside elevation remains unverified because the cached lake preview has flat elevation.

- Traffic near a route endpoint now checks newly loaded sector roads for a close, forward, legal continuation. Successful matches append the route and trim travelled waypoints to keep memory bounded; failed matches retry after five seconds, so dead ends still stop. Isolated Unity checks cover a newly streamed connecting road, a forbidden one-way continuation and the moving vehicle's refresh call. Real streamed driving and frame cost remain to profile on Android.

- An armed enemy under fire no longer downgrades to mere suspicion when it cannot find cover in open terrain; it makes a brief reposition attempt and advances to combat if cover is still unavailable. The isolated review suite now passes after supplying a road surface for its traffic fixture, initializing the alert fixture in Patrol, and fixing a randomly rotated skin-hit fixture.

- OSM requests now include forest, wood, scrub, meadow, grass and orchard polygons as well as parks. Landscape tags survive cache decoding, and park/woodland polygons crossing sector boundaries are clipped into the sector. Planting uses mapped land use: taller and denser woodland trees, more scrub undergrowth, sparser meadow trees, and no procedural plants on mapped sports pitches, while retaining mobile tree budgets and the existing road/building/water exclusions. A cached Naini Lake scene with an added forest polygon was rendered at ground level: the formerly open patch becomes woodland. Tree crowns use smaller overlapping clusters and lower gloss; shrubs use textured rounded meshes instead of triangular cards. The fixture rose from 656,813 to 825,629 triangles on desktop, so representative Android profiling is still required; the result remains procedural rather than photorealistic.

- Trees now use 128 m spatial mesh cells with nearby and simplified distant geometry, switched by camera distance and the existing mobile quality multiplier. In the isolated forest fixture, the overview enabled 28,720 distant-tree triangles and no nearby-tree triangles; the woodland close-up enabled 325,848 nearby and 5,200 distant triangles. The preview images retain trees at both distances, and code compilation and the isolated render pass. These are enabled mesh totals before frustum culling, not measured Android draw calls or FPS. Cell batching raises stored mesh count, so device profiling is still needed.

- Hilly mapped lakes now keep the immediate dry bank above the flat water surface when coarse elevation samples disagree with the shoreline, blending into the source terrain over 32 m. Player/enemy spawn searches require a dry standing footprint, and extraction zones require a dry, supported footprint with modest height variation across the full disc. A failed dustoff placement leaves the mission in progress with a retry notice instead of creating a floating or water-overlapping helicopter; VIP/courier setup falls back when no safe site exists. An isolated synthetic hillside lake verified submerged lake bed, raised bank, relocation from a water-center spawn, and rejection of a shoreline LZ; map-detail checks and 24 review regression groups passed. Live hilly lakes and Android playtesting remain unverified.

- Android scenery meshes now release their CPU-side vertex/index copy after upload. This applies only to visual vegetation and other collision-free scenery, preserving readable meshes for terrain, water and navigation. Code compilation and isolated map-detail checks passed; memory savings and loading impact still need measurement on a device.

- Nearby detailed buildings now select a ground-floor entrance on a street-facing wall when mapped roads are nearby, with a closed glazed door and small canopy. Mapped retail/commercial buildings use wider ground-floor shopfront glazing and a continuous fascia, while upper floors retain their existing windows; this reuses the existing facade meshes and materials. Tree planting now mixes rounded, spreading and slender crown proportions according to mapped land use, with matching near/distant shapes and no extra trees or triangles. The facade geometry check and cached urban/woodland sector renders passed. Entrances are visual and closed, and Android frame time remains unmeasured.

- Crossing generation now requires distinct mapped roads, so a right-angle bend within one road no longer paints a false zebra crossing. Curbs and road-edge paint check the length of each section for side-street overlap; curbs refine only near junctions to keep the visible edge close to narrow openings. The isolated map-detail regressions passed, and the 1,600-feature cached city render was inspected. Its mesh count stayed at 3,650 while triangles rose from 1,725,332 to 1,730,990 (about 0.3%) for the refined curbs; these desktop fixture figures are not Android performance measurements.

## Remaining before claiming realistic driving/world behavior
- Shopfronts now require a matched retail place category (or an explicit shop landmark without category), rather than generic commercial/office building classes. Facades and signs share footprint containment/nearest-wall matching within six metres, excluding road crossings and elevated building parts. Unmatched shops do not produce standalone shop signs. Compilation and isolated facade/category/host matching checks passed; live Overture locations still need visual review. Only buildings within the existing close-detail budget receive physical shopfront geometry.
- Building material/proportion correction: removed artificial repeating horizontal seams from default plaster, reduced oversized facade bands and surrounds, and placed finer glazing behind the outer trim with some divided panes. Compilation and the cached city render passed; the same street was visually compared. Buildings still need richer facade assets and building-type variation before claiming realistic appearance.
- Branch variation, regional driving side defaults and Overture direction restrictions, multiple lanes, mapped right-of-way and signals. Traffic still stops at true dead ends and where no matching road is loaded.
- Vehicle wheel rotation/steering, suspension and acceleration audio; route spawning must avoid visible pop-in. Shipped car prefabs did not expose separate named wheel meshes in the Unity model inventory, so wheel animation needs asset-level work.
- Real-location visual review of map geometry, material scale, road intersections and shoreline seams, including narrow rivers and water without stable source IDs.
- Wind response, species-specific leaf and bark assets, and more convincing close-range lighting; trees still use procedural meshes.
- Android profiling on representative devices and long-run thermal/memory checks. APK work remains deferred.

Synthetic fixture renders establish shader/geometry behavior, not final visual acceptance of a live city or lake.
